**1. short-058** (short, common_harakat, brackets) — Short ayah reported as not in the Mushaf

- Input: ﴿وَإِذَا الْأَرْضُ مُدَّتْ﴾
- Expected: `verified`, ref 84:3
- Got: `not_in_mushaf`, ref –, score 0.0
- Diagnosis: match_arabic returns not_found for any quote whose skeleton is under MIN_SKELETON = 10 letters (app/quran.py:20, app/quran.py:132), even inside ﴿﴾ and even with a correct written reference; check_quran (app/pipeline.py:225) then tells the reader the text was not found in the Mushaf. About a third of the ayat of <= 3 words are under 10 letters (﴿الله الصمد﴾، ﴿ويسر لي أمري﴾، ﴿والعصر﴾). The threshold protects free-text scanning; inside ﴿﴾ or with a reference it should not apply.

**2. substitution-086** (substitution, common, guillemets) — 'قال الله تعالى في سورة X:' puts 'في سورة X' into the quote

- Input: يقول الله تعالى في سورة ق: «يوم تشقق الأرض عنهم ينطقون ذلك حشر علينا يسير»
- Expected: `differs`, ref 50:44
- Got: `not_in_mushaf`, ref –, score 81.1; extracted quote «في سورة ق: «يوم تشقق الأرض عنهم ينطقون ذلك حشر علينا يسير»
- Diagnosis: QURAN_AR (app/extract.py:26) ends at 'تعالى'; _take_quote (app/extract.py:68) then skips only spaces and colons, finds no quotation mark at 'في', and takes the rest of the sentence. The quote becomes 'في سورة البقرة: «...' and is reported as 'differs' (or not found). With ﴿﴾ it is fine because the bracket rule runs first. Edit: substitute سِرَاعٗاۚ → يَنطِقُونَ at word 4.

**4. exact-031** (exact, common, guillemets) — Correct ayah in common spelling reported as misquoted

- Input: قال الله تعالى: «حقيق على أن لا أقول على الله إلا الحق قد جئتكم ببينة من ربكم فأرسل معي بني إسرائيل» (الأعراف: 105).
- Expected: `verified`, ref 7:105, reference_ok true
- Got: `differs`, ref 7:105, score 99.1, reference_given «(الأعراف: 105)», reference_ok true; diff: replace «إسرائيل»→«إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ»
- Diagnosis: Matching is an exact substring search on a consonantal skeleton (app/quran.py:134). skeleton_ar (app/normalize.py:37) only removes alef, hamza and marks, but the Uthmani rasm differs from standard spelling in letters too: hamza on a kursi or on the line where common spelling uses ئ/ؤ (شيئا، السيئات، إسرائيل، يؤوده، رؤوف), ىٰ inside a word (ضحىٰها → ضحاها، مأوىٰهم → مأواهم), small yaa/waw that are real letters (إبراهيم، النبيين، يحيي، يلوون), one-lam الليل/اللذين/اللاتي/اللائي, ننجي, يبسط. _DIACRITICS (app/normalize.py:12) deletes the small letters and _LETTER_MAP maps ئ→ي, ؤ→و, ى→ي, so the two spellings of the same word get different skeletons. The exact search fails and _fuzzy_arabic (app/quran.py:151) returns 'differs', with the correctly quoted word shown as a change. Gap words here: «إسرائيل» vs Mushaf «إِسۡرَـٰٓءِيلَ» (hamza_ya).

## Method notes and limits

- Distribution is synthetic: passages are uniform over ayat, not weighted toward the ayat people actually quote; edits are random words, not plausible slips. Rates are properties of this mix (stated in the script), not of real traffic. Per-style and per-marker tables let you re-weight.
- Common spelling comes from a rule-based converter (self-test in the script). Where hamza + waw has two accepted spellings it writes ؤ (رؤوف, يؤوده); those cases are tagged `hamza_waw` in the rule table above.
- Kinds 1–4 only use passages that occur once in the Mushaf, so the tool's handling of repeated ayat is not measured here (skipped while sampling: 14 draws).
- Misquote draws rejected to keep labels certain: insertion_donor 3, insertion_nearest_ambiguous 1, omission_nearest_ambiguous 7, omission_short_word 5, substitution_donor 9.
- Hadith lookup was off, so `not_in_mushaf` negatives were not cross-checked against the hadith encyclopedia; the label is about the Mushaf only.
