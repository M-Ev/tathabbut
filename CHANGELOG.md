# Changelog

## Build days (4 to 6 October 2026), evaluated

Starting point: `1bc66fb` (see STARTING_VERSION.md). Each entry names what was wrong before and what the tool does now.

### 4 October

- **Evidence status follows written rules (plan item 13, preflight B1).** Before: a hadith graded fabricated by al-Albani showed «حالة الدليل: موثّق المصدر» beside a red verdict, and the API returned `tier: documented`. After: the hadith tiers come from `data/display_rules.json` (team draft, awaiting the Sharia reviewer's signature, and the interface says so): `supported` (in the two Sahihs, or every matching grading accepted), `not_supported` (every matching grading weak or fabricated; red only for fabricated), `verify`, `refer` (orange, not red). `documented` now means only "the Quran quote matches the Mushaf". Tests: `test_fabricated_hadith_is_never_tagged_as_supported`, `test_weak_only_hadith_is_not_supported`.
- **A hadith listed with a bare «وقال:» is checked (preflight B2).** Before: in «وقال ﷺ: «...». وقال: «اطلبوا العلم ولو بالصين»» the second hadith was skipped silently. After: «وقال:», «وقال أيضًا:» and «وعنه:» right after a hadith in the same paragraph are extracted as hadith.
- **A reference without a colon ends the ayah (preflight B3).** Before: «إن الله مع الصابرين (البقرة 200)» became one quote and was called "not in the Mushaf". After: the ayah is found (al-Baqarah 153, al-Anfal 46) and the wrong number 200 is pointed out.
- **No distant hadith under a quote missing from the Mushaf (preflight B4).** Before: a Dorar result at 70% similarity was shown, with its grading, under the quote. After: only a strong match (85+) is shown there.
- **The two scholars' fatwas, verbatim (plan items 42-44, owner's decision 3 Oct).** Before: a personal fatwa question got three links (the two scholars' sites, then the official body). After: the tool searches each site with its own search (`app/fatwa.py`), keeps the fatwas whose title shares most of the question's words (at most three per scholar, floor 60%), and shows them as published: Ibn Baz's fatwa in full with source and link (the site allows copying with the source named); Ibn al-Uthaymeen's question and the opening line of the answer with its series (e.g. «فتاوى نور على الدرب، الشريط رقم [270]») and a link to the full fatwa on the foundation's site (its rights are reserved). The official fatwa body follows, named by its title instead of a bare address (preflight D6). ALLaM is not involved. Only the question's topic words are sent to the two sites. Tests: `test_fatwa_question_shows_the_two_scholars_fatwas_verbatim`, `test_fatwa_question_with_nothing_close_still_refers`. Docs: README, SOURCES_AND_LICENSES.md, docs/api-and-keys.md, architecture, operations.
- **Design notes from the preflight (D2-D5).** Referral is orange, never red (one colour, one meaning; done with item 13). Every stand-alone control is at least 44px tall on mobile (language switch 29→44, sample buttons 25→44, API link 16→44; measured at 390px). The correct Mushaf word is underlined instead of boxed in green, so the ayah stays unmarked. Arabic references inside English reasons are isolated (`<bdi dir="rtl">`), so «(البقرة: 154)» keeps its direction.
- **Quran matching that holds up to how people type (plan section أ, items 1-11).** Measured on the synthetic set (seed 2026, `eval/synth_report.md`). Before: correct Quran text typed in common spelling was called a misquote 52 times in 276 (18.8%), and 949 of the 6,216 ayat failed the common-spelling census (15.3%). After: 0 of 276, and 1 of 6,216; misquotes reported as differing 290 of 294 (was 213 of 234); a new class of 60 cases where only an alef or hamza was changed is caught 60 of 60; no misquote is ever called verified (0 before and after); median time per check 30 ms → 8 ms (the Mushaf now takes 2.0 s instead of 0.35 s to load). The common-spelling numbers are partly circular, since the second index and the cases use one converter; the report says so, and `tests/test_quran_spelling.py` covers 25 hand-typed ayat the converter did not write. What changed: a second Mushaf index in common spelling (`app/spelling.py`, moved out of the evaluation); an alef and hamza check between skeleton letters, so «ان» for «إِنَّ» stays correct but an added or dropped alef is a difference, with a partial word at the quote's edge tolerated; short marked ayat of two or three words matched as whole words, and a one-word quote said to be too short to check and referred to a Quran specialist; «في سورة X:» before a quote read as a reference; unmarked ayat in common spelling found; fuzzy windows cached and extended backwards at a surah's end, threshold 80 for quotes under 30 letters; a quote matching another riwaya named, not flagged (its wording awaits the Sharia reviewer); the small high hamza (U+065E) drawn with a glyph the Mushaf font has; text in Urdu, Persian, French, Indonesian or Turkish told plainly that v1 checks Arabic and English only.
- **Fatwa matching measured on live questions (plan item 45, first part).** Before: on 12 fatwa questions (`eval/fatwa_cases.jsonl`), 6 showed nothing; the question's words were compared without normalising, so «القرآن» never met «القران»; and a fatwa on removing body hair was shown for a new Muslim's question about his Christian wife, because its long question shared «حديثًا» and the spouse words. After: 3 of 12 show nothing (referral only), the hair fatwa is gone, and every question's top results are listed in `eval/fatwa_report.md` for the Sharia reviewer to mark. Changes: words normalised before comparison; a fatwa whose title shares no word with the question is not shown; each word weighted by how rare it is among the results (so «حائض» counts for more than «زوج»); Ibn Baz's search retried with the longest words when the full question finds too little; «لأني», «بسبب», «بدل», «فقط», «حديثًا» and similar treated as question frames. No hit rate is claimed until she marks the fitting fatwas. Test: `test_fatwa_ranking_needs_a_shared_word_in_the_title_and_weighs_rare_words`.
- **Live hadith check through the site, and no grading lent from a longer narration (plan items 15 and 12).** The hadith set (`eval/hadith_cases.jsonl`) was checked live on 4 Oct through the site's own `POST /api/check` (`eval/verify_hadith_cases.py --via-space`; Dorar refuses Claude's cloud directly): 30 of the 32 Arabic cases returned approved gradings, 2 none, and the 4 English cases wait for the model. The gradings are recorded as the site showed them, for the Sharia reviewer. The run found a real fault. Before: Ibn Hajar's «موضوع» on an 897-word sermon in «المطالب العالية» that contains «من غشنا فليس منا» was shown as a 100% match and turned this hadith of Sahih Muslim red, «لا تؤيده المصادر المعتمدة»; al-Dhahabi's «موضوع» on a 45-word narration did the same to «كن في الدنيا كأنك غريب» (al-Bukhari), and his «موضوع» on a 64-word sermon was lent to «حاسبوا أنفسكم قبل أن تحاسبوا»; and «الدين المعاملة» was shown beside al-Albani's «صحيح» on «إن الدين النصيحة». After: each grading says which wording it was said of. A narration more than twice the quote's length is marked «رواية أطول», shown with that note, and not counted; a quote with words missing from the narration is a similar wording, and the missing words are named (a quote of five words or fewer must be found whole; prepositions such as «في» / «بالـ» and a joined و are tolerated). A Sahih narration that contains the quote still counts. A fabricated grading of the same wording next to a Sahih narration gives «يحتاج مزيدًا من التحقق», never green or red. The values are in `data/display_rules.json` (coverage), awaiting the reviewer. Replayed on the 32 live results: the two Sahih hadith are «تؤيده المصادر» again; «حاسبوا أنفسكم» and «الكيس من دان نفسه» (graded only within longer narrations) need more checking instead of red; «تفكر ساعة» (the narrations say «فكرة ساعة ... ستين سنة») shows al-Albani's «موضوع» on the near wording and needs more checking. Tests: `test_grading_of_a_longer_narration_is_not_lent_to_a_short_quote`, `test_longer_narrations_only_need_more_checking`, `test_coverage_guard_shows_the_words_no_narration_has`.
- **The site says what it could not do (plan items 16 to 18).** Before: behind the Hugging Face proxy every visitor shared one limit of 20 checks per 10 minutes; `/api/health` did not say whether Dorar answers or which commit runs; and a text with more than 12 citations was cut with no word. After: the limit is per visitor (the last `X-Forwarded-For` entry, the one the proxy adds); `/api/health` gives `dorar_reachable`, the last success and error, `fatwa_search` and `version.commit`; when Dorar is down a banner says «الموسوعة الحديثية غير متاحة الآن: الآيات تُفحص كالمعتاد، والأحاديث تُحال إلى المختص حتى تعود»; the report says «فُحص أول ١٢ استشهادًا من ١٥» (and the same for characters), and the API returns `truncated`; too many requests get a plain message instead of «failed». Tests: `test_nothing_is_cut_silently`, `test_health_reports_dorar_and_the_rate_limit_sees_the_visitor`.
- **Written attribution read and compared (plan item 19).** Before: «رواه البخاري» after a fabricated saying passed unremarked, and «رُوي عن النبي ﷺ» was not read as a hadith marker. After: «رواه البخاري / مسلم / الشيخان», «متفق عليه», «في الصحيحين», «Sahih al-Bukhari», «(Bukhari and Muslim)» are read beside each hadith and compared with the Sahihayn line from Dorar's results: «كُتب في النص «رواه البخاري»، ولم نجده في صحيح البخاري ضمن نتائج البحث في الموسوعة الحديثية» (never «ليس في البخاري»); an attribution the search does not confirm turns «تؤيده المصادر» into «يحتاج مزيدًا من التحقق». A saying the sources do not support, written with «قال رسول الله ﷺ», gets the line «وما لم يثبت يُذكر بصيغة «رُوي» مع بيان حكمه» (wording awaits the Sharia reviewer); one written with «رُوي» does not. The «اختلفت الأحكام» line now counts only gradings of the same wording. Test: `test_written_attribution_is_compared_with_the_sahihayn_line`.
- **Sayings that borrow a hadith's grading, measured (plan item 14).** 20 sayings that change a word of a known narration or add to it (`eval/trap_cases.jsonl`, drafted by Claude for the reviewer), with the gradings the live site showed, scored before and after the coverage guard (`eval/trap_report.md`). Before: 13 of 20 were reported as found in that wording, e.g. «أحب الأعمال إلى الله أدومها وإن كثر» (the narration says «وإن قل») as al-Bukhari's «صحيح». After: 2 of 20, and one of them is al-Albani's «موضوع» on «حب الوطن من الإيمان» itself, so not a borrowing. The cost: of 27 hadith in the hadith set reported in their wording before, 3 now show as a similar wording (the narration is abbreviated in Dorar or worded differently). This needed a quote of up to 10 words (not 5) to be found whole; chosen on these same cases, so the reviewer's set is the real test (`data/display_rules.json`).
- **The model's part is visible and bound (plan items 21 and 22, before ALLaM's server is connected).** Before: the «استعن بعلّام» option could be ticked while no model ran, and nothing said whether the model took part; the model's reply was parsed as free text; the checked text went into the prompt as plain text. After: the option is switched off with its reason when the model server is off («الفحص المعمّق بعلّام غير متاح الآن...»), and every report says «استُعين بعلّام في هذا الفحص (n ث)» or «لم يُستعن بالنموذج اللغوي» (the API returns `model.used`, `calls`, `seconds`). Every model call asks for a fixed JSON shape (`response_format` json_schema on an OpenAI-compatible server such as vLLM, a JSON grammar on llama.cpp; a server that refuses it is asked again without it, and the shape is always checked locally). The checked text is passed as a marked data block the text cannot close, and the model is told it holds no instructions. For an English hadith the model's pick of an Arabic narration is accepted only if it shares the Arabic wording the model itself proposed (60% floor). Tests: `test_injected_instructions_in_the_text_change_nothing`, `test_model_pick_must_share_its_own_wording`.
- **A decision for chatbots, and a page that shows it (plan items 24, 25 and 33).** Before: `/api/check` returned statuses only; an integrator had to decide what to do, could not place a note at the citation, and could not tell whether everything was checked. After: every reply carries `decision` (`pass`, `annotate` or `block`, with the reason per citation) from `data/chatbot_policy.json`, a written policy an operator can edit; `citations[].span` (position in the text sent); `coverage` (`complete` is false when the text or the citations were cut, a source was unreachable or a language is unsupported, and such an answer never passes); `disclaimer`; and `versions` (commit, rules status, policy version, model). The tool never rewrites the answer (`rewrites_answer: false`). `docs/api.md` has 20-line examples in Python and JavaScript, and `/static/chatbot.html` shows an answer as the chatbot wrote it beside what its user would see. Under «نتيجة الفحص» the report now says the tool is AI-assisted, not a scholar or a mufti, and checked the citations only, not the explanation or the reasoning. Test: `test_chatbot_decision_follows_the_policy_and_never_passes_a_partial_check`.
- **A report a da'i can work from (plan items 27 and 28).** Before: the results were a list of cards with no link back to the text, and nothing could be copied with its source. After: the text as pasted appears above the cards, each quoted passage marked with an icon, its number and a word for its status (never colour alone), linked to its card; the rest is labelled as the writer's own words, not checked. Each card has «انسخ الاستشهاد بمصدره», built from the source fields only: an ayah in the Mushaf text with [السورة: الآية], a hadith as «...» رواه مسلم (رقم) with its Dorar link when it is in the Sahihayn, otherwise the narration with each grading verbatim and its scholar; it is disabled, with the reason, for a similar wording, a quote not found, or an incomplete check. The report head gives the Hijri date and the version, and says it covers the citations listed only. The API gives `quote_span` beside `span`.
- **The fatwa detector reads real cases (plan item 32).** Before: «هل علي بن أبي طالب أول من أسلم من الصبيان؟» was taken as a fatwa question, while «أنا طلقت زوجتي وهي حائض» and a chatbot's «نعم يجوز لك أن تفعل ذلك» were missed. After: «هل علي» counts only before a word of obligation (أن، إثم، كفارة، قضاء، زكاة...); first-person cases (أنا طلقت، حلفت بالطلاق، نذرت أن، زوجي طلقني، ماذا أفعل إذا...) and rulings on the asker's own case in an answer (يجوز لك، يجب عليك، طلاقك واقع، صلاتك باطلة; in English «yes, you can», «your divorce is valid») are level د. An answer that rules is told so («في النص حكم على حالة شخصية بعينها...»), and the two scholars' sites are searched with the question it replied to. The API gives `level_d.form` (`question` or `ruling_in_answer`). Test: `test_fatwa_detector_reads_first_person_cases_and_rulings_in_answers`.
- **The scientific package, row by row (plan items 30 and 31).** Before: conformance was claimed in prose. After: `docs/package-conformance.md` has a row for each of the eight output standards (p. 5), each content level (p. 2), each reference domain (pp. 3-4) and each test case on p. 6, with what the tool does and the test that shows it; rows that do not apply to a checker say why. `tests/test_package_conformance.py` covers the p. 6 cases that apply (the personal marriage case, «أعطني حديثًا يثبت هذا الكلام», «هل كل المسلمين يتفقون», the misquoted ayah, the non-Arabic term) and the transparency, privacy and unsupported-language standards: only the hadith wording reaches Dorar, never a name or phone number in the text.
- **Results a judge can rerun (plan item 34).** Before: the published `eval/report.md` was stale and showed the four hadith cases as "source error", and no single command reran the measures. After: `eval/run_eval.py --via-space` evaluates the live site and the report says where and when it ran (12/12 traced, 2/2 correct abstentions on 4 Oct, on the site's build before tonight's pushes); `scripts/eval_all.sh [site]` runs the tests and every measure; `eval/ERRATA.md` lists every fault found in the build days with the commit that fixed it and its numbers; the home page has «جرّب حالات الاختبار», eight cases that each run with one tap and state the expected result.

- **Urdu and Indonesian verse quotes (plan item 36).** Before: Urdu or Indonesian text was said to be unsupported and nothing in it was checked. After: a verse quoted in Urdu or Indonesian is matched against the King Fahd Complex translation of that language (Junagarhi's Urdu, book 1966; the Complex's Indonesian with Indonesia's Ministry of Religious Affairs, book 1961), fetched from quranpedia.net with a pinned sha256 that is checked at every start, and shown in the Mushaf's wording with the translation beside it and its link. Measured by `eval/translation_census.py` on 900 quotes per language: 869 (96.6%) Urdu and 858 (95.3%) Indonesian traced to their ayah, 15 and 24 to another ayah, and none of 18 invented sentences per language tied to a verse. Ranking by word overlap alone had tied an invented Urdu sentence to 58:22 at 81.9; the score now also requires the words in order. Urdu and Indonesian citation markers («اللہ تعالیٰ فرماتا ہے», «رسول اللہ ﷺ نے فرمایا», «Allah SWT berfirman», «Rasulullah SAW bersabda») and «(QS. 94:5)» references are read; a speaker of each language should review them. A hadith in either language is referred and counted as unchecked: there is no approved hadith translation to match it against and the model covers only Arabic and English. French waits on the team's choice of translation.
